Cybersecurity Analyst

Reference Number: SEC918

Salary: $115,000 – $130,000 per annum + Super

Reporting Line: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Protect our financial platform by continuously monitoring threats, assessing vulnerabilities, and enforcing security compliance across all operational systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitoring networks and systems for security breaches, intrusions, and anomalous activity.
  • Conduct routine vulnerability assessments and coordinate penetration testing.
  • Develop, maintain, and execute comprehensive incident response procedures.
  • Ensure platform adherence to security frameworks like PCI-DSS.

Requirements

Essential Requirements
  1. 3+ years in a specialized Information Security or Security Operations role.
  2. Strong understanding of cryptographic protocols, network protocols, and firewalls.
  3. Relevant security certification (e.g., CISSP, CISM, or CompTIA Security+).
Preferable Requirements
  • Degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, or Information Technology.
  • Hands-on experience with SIEM tools and threat monitoring tools

Financial Data Analyst

Reference Number: DAT551

Salary: $95,000 – $110,000 per annum + Super

Reporting Line: Analytics Manager

Translate customer transaction data and financial trends into actionable business insights and drive product strategies, fraud prevention models, and user engagement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Extract, transform, and analyze complex datasets to identify spending behavior patterns.
  • Design interactive data dashboards and reporting systems for executive stakeholders.
  • Partner with product teams to validate hypotheses using A/B testing methodologies.
  • Assist risk management teams with fraud detection trend analysis.

Requirements

Essential Requirements
  1. 2+ years of experience in Data Analytics or Business Intelligence.
  2. Advanced proficiency with SQL and statistical tools.
  3. Solid foundation in statistical analysis and predictive modeling techniques.
Preferable Requirements
  • Background in quantitative finance of consumer lending metrics.
  • Familiarity with automated data pipeline technologies.
